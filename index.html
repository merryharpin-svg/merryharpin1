About

Merry Harpin is a self-taught artist living and working in Norwich, Connecticut. She grew up along the Mississippi Gulf Coast. Her studio practice is centered around charcoal, while she also explores oils, watercolors, and acrylics.

Her work is guided by the sacred dimensions of the everyday, deeply rooted in beauty. For her, recognizing beauty is not simply a matter of preference, but an intentional act of attention. When she is not in the studio, she nurtures this focus in nature - her greatest teacher and mentor.
