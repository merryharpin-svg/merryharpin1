About

Merry Harpin is a self-taught artist living and working in Norwich, Connecticut. Originally from the Mississippi Gulf Coast, her practice centers on charcoal, with ongoing work in oil, watercolor, and acrylic.

The sacred dimensions of the everyday guide her work. She has developed a strong artistic discipline rooted in close observation of natural forms, ordinary objects, and the spaces we inhabit. When she is not in the studio, she finds her greatest mentorship and teaching from nature, moving through the empirical world with a sense of wonder.
